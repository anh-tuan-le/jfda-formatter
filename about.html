What it does

Reads the submission

PDF, DOCX, PPTX, Markdown, plain text, CSV, JSON and HTML. It works from paragraph styles rather than font sizes, so it still finds the structure in exports that have none — SSRN downloads, authors glued into one line, unnumbered sections, captions attached to the wrong paragraph.

Rebuilds it to template

Front matter, authors and affiliations, abstract, keywords, JEL codes, numbered sections, tables, figures and back matter are placed into the journal's own .docx template. Sections renumber to match. A live PDF preview shows the result as you work.

Fixes the citations

Numeric citations convert to author–date. Legal instruments keep their short forms. Reference lists rewrite to APA 7 with real italics on journal names and volume numbers, and in-text citations then sync to match the list. Every change is shown for approval before it is applied.

Bring your own key

The AI features need an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. Your key is stored in your own browser and calls go directly from your browser to the provider — it is never sent to this server and never shared with other editors. Parsing, template rebuilding and export all work without a key.
